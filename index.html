Why ProperTickets?

Real tickets. Real fans. Fair prices.

Buying a ticket to see an artist you love should be fair, transparent and secure. We're developing an independent UK ticketing platform that puts that principle first.

ProperTickets is currently in development and is not yet selling tickets.

Our approach

Ticketing built around fairness.

Face-value resale

Resale tickets sold at exactly their original face value, without seller-selected markups or discounts.

Transparent fees

Clear buyer-paid service and secure transfer fees, designed to cover costs and keep the platform sustainable.

Secure ownership transfer

Building a safer ticket transfer experience that protects fans and supports artists and venues.
